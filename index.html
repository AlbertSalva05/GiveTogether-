The platform

Four things that decide whether a campaign gets funded

We shipped the platform after two years of running relief drives on spreadsheets. Each feature below exists because a campaign stalled without it.

  • Identity verification

    Government ID, barangay certification and bank ownership checked before a page can accept a peso. Median turnaround is two working days.

  • Payments donors already use

    GCash, Maya, Visa, Mastercard and 26 banks in one checkout. 3-D Secure on every card, and the total on the button is the total charged.

  • Receipted tranche payouts

    Funds release in stages against uploaded receipts, and the ledger is public on the campaign page. Donors stop asking where the money went.

  • Donor reporting that exports

    Every donation, message and refund as CSV or JSON, plus a read-only API for your own dashboard. Board reports take minutes, not a weekend.

How it works

From sign-up to first payout in three steps

No sales call required on the Community plan. The slowest part is verification, and we tell you exactly what to upload.

  1. 1

    Build the page

    Write the story, set the goal, and break the budget into line items. Templates exist for medical, disaster relief, education and livelihood.

    ≈ 25 minutes

  2. 2

    Get verified

    Upload one government ID, a barangay or SEC document, and the bank account that will receive payouts. We check ownership, not paperwork volume.

    ≈ 2 working days

  3. 3

    Collect and release

    Donations land instantly; payouts release per tranche once receipts are in. Donors see each release on the page without being emailed.

    First payout ≈ 36 hrs
